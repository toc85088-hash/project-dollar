# Cleaning SEO / Traffic Foundation — Sprint 2

Research/check date: **2026-10-08**. Market target: US residential cleaning owners. Naveliqo remains the parent brand; Cleaning is the only active vertical. This map supplements, not replaces, `Keyword.md` and its preserved generic briefs.

## Evidence and limits

Nine requested seed themes were searched, with residential/US variants where needed. Results are **English-language search observations for a US-targeted editorial decision**, not a controlled US-local Google ranking study. Some results concern Australia, commercial janitorial work, or unrelated data cleaning; those are not evidence of US residential demand. Result order is not recorded as a ranking.

Search volume, CPC, keyword difficulty, traffic potential, conversion rates and competition scores: **NOT VERIFIED**. No paid keyword tool, account login or invented numeric estimate was used. Intent classifications below are editorial inferences from the observed result types, not measured audience research. Vendor pages demonstrate how a query is addressed; their marketing claims are not adopted as facts.
