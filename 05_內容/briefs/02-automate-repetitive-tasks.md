# Content Brief 02 — Automate Repetitive Tasks

Status: Needs keyword/SERP validation, workflow testing, editorial review, and human publication approval.

## Primary keyword

`how to automate repetitive tasks in a small business`

## Search intent

How-to / problem solving. The reader already feels the pain of repetitive work and wants a sequence that does not require code.

## Article purpose

Teach a reusable process for selecting, mapping, testing, and maintaining one automation. Prevent readers from choosing a tool before understanding the task.

## Target reader

A time-poor small business owner or operations lead who can describe the manual task but has no automation experience.

## Suggested title

How to Automate Repetitive Tasks in a Small Business (Without Coding)

## Suggested URL
