# Content Brief 03 — Small Business Automation Ideas

Status: Needs keyword/SERP validation, example verification, editorial review, and human publication approval.

## Primary keyword

`small business automation ideas`

## Search intent

Inspirational and informational. The reader wants relevant ideas but also needs help separating useful opportunities from risky or uneconomic ones.

## Article purpose

Offer a curated decision guide, not a generic list. Group ideas by business task, state what remains human, and give a simple first-choice filter.

## Target reader

A non-technical owner exploring automation who is unsure where it could fit in daily operations.

## Suggested title

Small Business Automation Ideas: What to Automate First

## Suggested URL

`/workflows/small-business-automation-ideas`

## H1

Small Business Automation Ideas That Start with a Real Workflow

## Suggested outline

### H2: How to use this list

- H3: Choose by task frequency and risk
- H3: Keep a person responsible for the outcome

### H2: Customer enquiry ideas

- Classify enquiries, prepare reply drafts, route urgent issues

### H2: Sales follow-up ideas

- Create reminders, draft follow-ups, update a review queue

### H2: Meetings and admin ideas

- Prepare agendas, draft notes, extract action items, file approved records

### H2: Marketing operations ideas

- Repurpose an approved source, prepare variations, quality-review before scheduling

### H2: Finance-adjacent admin ideas

- Flag missing invoice information or prepare reminders; never automate financial judgment without qualified review

### H2: Ideas not to start with

- Sensitive decisions, customer promises, hiring decisions, payments, or complex exception-heavy processes

### H2: A scorecard for choosing one idea

- Frequency, clarity, risk, data sensitivity, measurability, and owner
