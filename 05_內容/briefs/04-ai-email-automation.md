# Content Brief 04 — AI Email Automation

Status: Needs keyword/SERP validation, end-to-end testing, privacy review, editorial review, and human publication approval.

## Primary keyword

`AI email automation for small business`

## Search intent

Solution seeking. The reader wants to reduce time spent sorting or drafting routine business email without losing control of customer communication.

## Article purpose

Show a safe email workflow that classifies an incoming message, prepares a draft from approved information, and requires a person to review before sending.

## Target reader

A small business owner or office manager handling a shared inbox and recurring enquiry types.

## Suggested title

## Suggested outline

### H2: What this workflow does—and does not do

- Classify, retrieve approved notes, draft, flag uncertainty, route to review

### H2: Decide which messages qualify

- H3: Routine, low-risk enquiries
- H3: Messages that must bypass automation

### H2: Map the workflow

- Trigger, fields, approved knowledge, draft, confidence/exception, reviewer, send

- H3: Remove customer information
- H3: Define prohibited promises and escalation words

### H2: Build the draft-and-review flow

- Tool-neutral steps first; named implementation only after real testing

### H2: Test failure cases

- Ambiguous request, angry customer, sensitive data, attachment, prompt injection, missing knowledge, duplicate message

### H2: Measure and maintain

- Draft acceptance, review time, wrong routing, missed escalation, knowledge freshness

### H2: When not to use AI email automation

- Legal threats, safety issues, refunds/commitments, sensitive records, or no accountable reviewer
