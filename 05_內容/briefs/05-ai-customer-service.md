# Content Brief 05 — AI Customer Service

Status: Needs keyword/SERP validation, service workflow testing, expert review where needed, editorial review, and human publication approval.

## Primary keyword

`AI customer service for small business`

## Search intent

Informational / commercial investigation. The reader is considering AI to handle support volume but needs practical boundaries, workflow options, and tool-selection criteria.

## Article purpose

Help an owner design a triage-and-draft workflow that improves consistency while protecting escalation, empathy, and accountability.

## Target reader

A small retailer, service business, agency, or ecommerce operator with recurring support questions and limited staff.

## Suggested title

AI Customer Service for Small Business: Start with Triage, Not a Bot

## Suggested outline

### H2: The best first role for AI in support

- Triage and drafting rather than replacing the support owner

### H2: Map your support categories and risk

- H3: Routine information requests
- H3: Billing, refunds, complaints, safety, and sensitive issues

### H2: Build an approved knowledge source

- H3: What belongs in it
- H3: Ownership, versioning, and expiration

### H2: Design the triage workflow

- Intake, category, urgency, knowledge retrieval, draft, escalation, review, reply

### H2: Test for helpfulness and harm

- Unsupported answer, wrong policy, missing context, hostile input, vulnerable customer, personal data

### H2: Measure customer and operational outcomes

- First response workflow time, escalation quality, correction rate, repeat contact; avoid claiming causal gains without data

### H2: Choose a tool only after the workflow is clear

- Data controls, channels, human handoff, logs, export, support, and total work

### H2: Maintenance checklist

- Knowledge owner, policy updates, sampled review, incident process, retirement trigger
