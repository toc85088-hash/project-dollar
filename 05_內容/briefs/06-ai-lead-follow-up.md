# Content Brief 06 — AI Lead Follow-Up Automation

Status: Needs keyword/SERP validation, end-to-end testing, consent/privacy review, editorial review, and human publication approval.

## Primary keyword

`AI lead follow up automation`

## Search intent

Solution seeking / commercial investigation. The reader wants faster, more consistent follow-up without sending inaccurate or unwanted messages.

## Article purpose

Teach a draft-and-approve workflow from captured enquiry to scheduled human-reviewed follow-up, including consent, exceptions, suppression, and measurement.

## Target reader

A small service business, agency, consultant, or local business that receives enquiries through forms or email and follows up manually.

## Suggested title

AI Lead Follow-Up Automation: A Small-Business Workflow with Human Approval

## Suggested URL

`/workflows/ai-lead-follow-up-automation`

## H1

AI Lead Follow-Up Automation Without Losing the Personal Check

## Suggested outline

### H2: What to automate in lead follow-up

- Capture, enrich from provided data, prepare a draft, set a reminder, log a decision
- Keep qualification judgment, promises, and sending approval human in the starter workflow

### H2: Define a valid lead and valid permission

- H3: Required fields and source
- H3: Consent, suppression, opt-out, and market differences requiring qualified review

### H2: Map the workflow

- Trigger, context, routing, draft rules, reviewer, send/schedule, CRM/log, stop condition

### H2: Prepare message guardrails

- Approved facts, tone, prohibited claims, personalization limits, escalation

### H2: Build and test with fictional leads

- Missing fields, duplicate, existing customer, out-of-region, opt-out, ambiguous request, sensitive data

### H2: Review before every send

- Accuracy, recipient, consent state, promise, tone, links, next action

### H2: Measure the workflow honestly

- Review time, correction rate, delivery status, opt-outs, qualified responses; do not imply revenue causation without evidence

### H2: Maintain and stop the automation

- Ownership, template changes, suppression sync, incident response
