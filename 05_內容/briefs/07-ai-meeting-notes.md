# Content Brief 07 — AI Meeting Notes

Status: Needs keyword/SERP validation, tool/workflow testing, participant-consent review, editorial review, and human publication approval.

## Primary keyword

`AI meeting notes for small business`

## Search intent

Informational / solution seeking. The reader wants usable notes and action items without spending meeting time typing, but is concerned about accuracy and privacy.

## Article purpose

Explain a consent-aware workflow for capture, draft notes, verification, action-owner confirmation, and approved storage.

## Target reader

A small business owner or team lead running recurring internal or client meetings and currently writing notes manually.

### H2: Define the output before choosing a tool

- Decisions, action, owner, due date, unresolved question, and source timestamp

### H2: The review-first workflow

- Permission, capture, transcript, draft, source check, participant/owner confirmation, approved storage, deletion

### H2: Test common failure cases

- Speaker confusion, names, numbers, jargon, accents, side conversations, poor audio, missed disagreement

### H2: Review notes efficiently

- Verify decisions and commitments first; never treat a summary as the authoritative record without review

### H2: Choose a tool

- Capture method, language/accent support, participant experience, export, retention, deletion, access, pricing

### H2: Maintain the process

- Consent script, storage owner, retention schedule, correction path, tool-change review
