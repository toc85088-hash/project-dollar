# Content Brief 10 — Small Business Automation Templates

Status: Needs keyword/SERP validation, template usability testing, accessibility QA, editorial review, and human publication approval.

## Primary keyword

`small business automation templates`

## Search intent

Resource / action. The reader wants reusable documents that help plan or test an automation without starting from a blank page.

## Article purpose

Create a template hub that turns the first nine articles into concrete, vendor-neutral tools. Explain when and how to use each template; avoid an empty download wall.

## Target reader

A non-technical owner who has identified a repetitive task and wants a structured way to plan, test, or compare an automation.

## Potential affiliate opportunities

None required on the download action. Optional tool links may appear in the usage guidance only after testing and disclosure. Do not gate a supposedly free template behind a paid affiliate action.

## Evidence/testing required

- Have at least three representative target readers use each template on a fictional or non-sensitive task; record real feedback without fabricating participants or quotes.
- Verify calculations with documented sample cases and edge cases.
- Test editable and printable formats, screen-reader reading order, labels, contrast, and instructions.
- Have a qualified reviewer inspect risk/privacy wording where applicable.

## Screenshots required

- Accurate preview of every downloadable template.
- One filled example using clearly fictional data.
- No mock download button or preview for a file that does not exist.

## Claims requiring verification

- Time saved, completeness, universal applicability, calculation results, compatibility, accessibility conformance, number of users/testers, and any tool-specific instructions.
