## Search intent

Learn / prevent: the reader wants to avoid expensive, risky, or unmaintainable automation decisions before connecting another tool.

## Article purpose

Give a non-technical owner a practical risk review that exposes missing process definition, excessive authority, unnecessary data access, weak testing, superficial human review, hidden failures, incomplete measurement, and loss of manual recovery.

## Target reader

A small-business owner or operations lead who is considering a first AI-assisted workflow or auditing an early automation that has not yet proved reliable.

## Suggested outline

- Direct answer: authority should never exceed evidence
- Nine mistakes, each with a corrective action
  - Automating an unclear process
  - Starting from software rather than the business task
  - Giving an early workflow external authority
  - Granting more data access than the task needs
  - Testing only the normal case
  - Treating a click-through approval as meaningful oversight
  - Hiding retries, exceptions, and partial failures
  - Measuring only the automated step
  - Removing the manual fallback
- Original BOUNDARY review framework
- When the manual process is better
