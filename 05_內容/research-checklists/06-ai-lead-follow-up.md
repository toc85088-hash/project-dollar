# Research Checklist 06 — AI Lead Follow-Up

對應 brief：`06-ai-lead-follow-up.md`  
狀態：Consent/deliverability risk；testing not started。

## Claims requiring verification

- Form capture、validation、qualification boundary、drafting、CRM update、scheduling、suppression 與 human approval。
- Conversion、response rate、speed-to-lead、time saved、deliverability 或 revenue claim。
- Integration、plan、price、data retention/training、permissions、consent、unsubscribe 與 market availability。

## Tools requiring testing

- Test form、test mailbox、non-production CRM、scheduling/automation tools（只選 workflow 真正需要者）。
- 不使用真實 leads，不對外寄送；測 duplicate prevention、suppression、owner assignment、logging 與 disable。

## Pricing requiring verification

- CRM seats/contacts、form submissions、automation tasks、email sends、AI usage、scheduling、premium connector、overage。
- Official source、checked date、market/currency、tax、billing period、trial/cancellation。

## Screenshots needed

- Lead follow-up workflow、test form、fictional records、review queue。
- Field mapping、automation rule、draft、suppression/unsubscribe、duplicate handling、log/failure、disable settings。
- 遮蔽 workspace/account identifiers；不得顯示真實 contact details。

## Workflow test needed

- Fictional submissions：complete, missing, duplicate, invalid email, ambiguous service, opt-out, restricted/sensitive content, prompt injection, after-hours 與 integration failure。
- 驗證不自行承諾 price/availability、不覆蓋 source data、不聯絡 suppressed record、保留 human send approval。
- 記錄 manual baseline、corrections、deliverability test boundary、retry duplication、maintenance。

## Affiliate opportunity

- Medium–High；CRM、form、scheduling、email、automation。
- 只連實際 workflow 使用且測過的最低必要 tools；說明 native/manual alternative 與 total stack burden。

## Internal links

- `/workflows`、`/tutorials`、`/ai-tools`、`/templates`、`/affiliate-disclosure`。
- 發布後可連 email automation、repetitive tasks；draft/blocked comparisons 不連。

## External evidence required

- Vendor official integration/API, billing, permissions, privacy/security, retention/training, deliverability/support docs。
- Market-specific marketing/email consent、privacy、unsubscribe 與 record-keeping authoritative sources。
- Conversion/response statistics 需 original methodology 與 matching context；否則刪除。

## What cannot be claimed without first-hand testing

- Increased conversion/revenue、faster response、better deliverability、correct qualification、no duplicates。
- Integration quality、setup time、field mapping reliability、suppression behavior、human approval 或 total cost。
- 法律 compliant 或適合所有 leads/markets。

## Publication stop gate

- Non-production end-to-end test、suppression/duplicate/failure evidence、consent review、pricing check、human approval 未完成前不得發布。
