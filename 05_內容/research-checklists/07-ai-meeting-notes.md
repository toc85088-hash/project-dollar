# Research Checklist 07 — AI Meeting Notes

對應 brief：`07-ai-meeting-notes.md`  
狀態：Recording/consent risk；testing not started。

## Claims requiring verification

- Transcription、speaker separation、summary、action item、export、integration 與 deletion behavior。
- Accuracy、accent/language support、time saved、real-time capability、retention/model training、security、consent。
- Plan/price/free limits、meeting length、participant limits、storage、export format 與 regional availability。

## Tools requiring testing

- Manual/native meeting feature 與一個 dedicated meeting assistant，前提是可合法取得並同意測試。
- 只使用 scripted fictional meeting，所有參與者知情同意；不用真實 customer/staff meeting。
- 測 setup、join behavior、recording indicators、permissions、correction、export/delete 與 failure。

## Pricing requiring verification

- Per user/seat、meeting/minute limits、AI summaries、storage、integrations、team/admin features、trial、annual/monthly、tax/cancellation。
- 發布當日 official pricing/docs，標示 checked date、plan、market/currency。

## Screenshots needed

- Meeting-notes workflow、fictional agenda/transcript、consent notice example。
- Recording/privacy settings、speaker transcript、summary/action items、correction、export/delete、failure state。
- 不顯示真實 names、emails、calendar、meeting IDs 或 recordings。

## Workflow test needed

- Scripted fictional meeting 包含多 speakers、口音/語速變化、overlap、專有名詞、否定句、責任人、due date、敏感片段與 prompt injection text。
- 驗證 consent/indicator、speaker attribution、action item accuracy、correction、export/delete 與 manual fallback。
- 可重複比較 native/manual/dedicated 方法；記錄未測 languages/conditions。

## Affiliate opportunity

- Medium–High；meeting assistant 或 collaboration tool。
- 只有實測且 relationship active 才用 affiliate link；privacy/consent limitation 必須在 CTA 前可見。

## Internal links

- `/workflows`、`/ai-tools`、`/tutorials`、`/templates`、`/affiliate-disclosure`。
- 發布後可連 tool evaluation article，但只能是已測且已發布版本。

## External evidence required

- Vendor official language/accent support、retention/training、security/privacy、recording indicator、integration/export/delete、billing docs。
- Recording/consent/employment/privacy 的 market-specific authoritative sources；需專業 review，不能只寫通用 disclaimer。

## What cannot be claimed without first-hand testing

- Accuracy、speaker recognition、summary/action-item correctness、setup ease、time saved、export reliability。
- 某口音/語言表現、meeting platform integration、data deletion 或 admin control。
- Tool 是「safe」「compliant」或 participants 接受錄音。

## Publication stop gate

- Fictional consented test、comparison log、retention/training fact check、recording-law review、screenshots、human approval 未完成前不得發布推薦。
