Article：**What Should a Small Business Automate First?**  
Audit date：2026-08-30  
Version audited：Production-quality draft plus evidence-completion boundary review, not published
Publication decision：**HOLD**

## Audit checks

### Generic AI filler

**PASS with minor human review.** The draft does not explain broad AI history, speculate about future disruption or add generic benefit lists. It states that the first useful automation may not need AI.

### Unsupported claims

**PASS for draft / NOT READY for publication.** Product capability statements are limited to official documentation. Unsupported outcome statements are explicitly excluded. Three marker types remain because the actual workflow and screenshots do not exist.

### Fake experience

**PASS.** The text says no account connection, run or screenshot has been completed. It never says “we tested” or describes an owner/customer result.

### Duplicated competitor structure

**PASS with caveat.** Common SERP sections—short answer, good candidates, what not to automate and FAQ—are necessary for intent. The FIRST veto rule, evidence boundary, selected workflow and full-process measurement provide a distinct sequence and rationale. No competitor wording was copied.

### Excessive keyword repetition

**PASS.** The exact query appears in H1/title and natural explanatory context. Headings use reader questions and action language instead of repeating the keyword.

### Unnecessary length

**PASS pending human trim.** Each section answers a separate decision or risk question. The candidate section and human-review section may be shortened after a beginner read-through if they feel repetitive.

### Vague advice

**PASS.** The draft provides score definitions, two veto rules, a six-field process map, a selected tool stack, test cases and measurement fields. Real test results remain intentionally absent.

### Commercial bias

**PASS.** Make and Google are presented as a planned demonstration stack with limits and permission risks, not as “best” products. Zapier appears only in research/test rationale. No vendor CTA is present.

### Affiliate bias

**PASS.** No affiliate relationship, affiliate link, commission CTA or disguised recommendation exists.

### Hidden assumptions

**NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW.** The demonstration assumes the approved tester has access to Google and Make, can grant suitable OAuth permissions, and finds the visual scenario understandable. Account type, region, admin policy, accessibility and real-business fit have not been validated.

### Unresolved evidence requirements

**BLOCKS PUBLICATION.** Current draft includes:

- `[TEST REQUIRED]`
- `[SCREENSHOT REQUIRED]`
- `[VERIFY CURRENT PLAN]`

`[SOURCE REQUIRED]` is part of the centralized guard but is not currently used in the draft because included factual claims have sources or were removed.

## Required improvements before publication

1. Human editor confirms FIRST name, scoring language and veto explanation are clear and sufficiently distinctive.
2. Approved tester performs the controlled fictional-data workflow test and preserves failures.
3. Capture/redact real screenshots or remove claims that require them.
4. Recheck Make plan, modules, Google behavior and official guidance on the publication date.
5. Update Claim Ledger from real results; remove or rewrite every unsupported sentence.
6. Assign a real author/editor and complete fact check.
7. Resolve every editorial marker; automated guard must then pass with zero markers.
8. Confirm destination internal links are published/content-ready before adding public links.
9. Complete human legal/privacy wording review for the intended markets and workflow scope.
10. Approve publication explicitly; until then `status: draft` and `humanApproved: false` remain unchanged.

## Evidence completion round recheck

Recheck date：2026-08-30

- Existing Google/Make workflow selection remains coherent and was not changed.
- Official documentation remains useful for limited capability statements.
- The exact workflow could not be run without Google and Make login/OAuth approval.
- No account was accessed, no paid trial was started, no data was submitted and no screenshot was captured.
- Claims marked `TESTED`：0.
- Unresolved marker types remain `[TEST REQUIRED]`、`[SCREENSHOT REQUIRED]` and `[VERIFY CURRENT PLAN]`.
- Draft content was not changed because no new first-hand result supports a factual improvement.

Score decision：**all scores remain unchanged**. In particular, Evidence quality remains **6/10** and Publication readiness remains **4/10**. Documentation planning alone is not a reason to raise either score.

## Final quality judgement

The draft is **safe and useful for human editorial review**, but **not safe to publish**. Its strongest value is the FIRST risk veto and transparent untested demonstration. Its weakest area is first-hand evidence, which cannot be improved without approved external login, a real controlled test and real screenshots.
