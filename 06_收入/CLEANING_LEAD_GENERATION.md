## Manual discovery sequence

1. Owner confirms real sender identity, monitored business email and approved pilot scope.
2. Choose the first business based on a relevant question, not a scraped contact list. Check its published contact purpose; a homeowner quote form is not an authorized sales channel.
3. Obtain explicit authority for any outbound message. Do not send from this sprint.
4. Ask permission for a short workflow conversation, without claiming affiliation, existing clients, results, or a detected defect.
5. Record response, fit, existing tools, actual problem, decision-maker consent and next action. “No reply” is not interest. Respect opt-out; do not automate follow-up.
6. Before real outreach, review applicable email/privacy rules and required sender/disclosure details against current official sources. No legal compliance approval is asserted here.
