## Demonstration workflow selected

**YES.** Planned workflow:

`fictional Google Form lead → linked Google Sheet → Make required-field/exception filter → Gmail acknowledgement draft (no send) → Google Tasks owner follow-up → Sheet status log → human review`

The workflow is intentionally rule-based. It demonstrates that a first useful automation may not need AI. A later bounded AI-assisted classification test can be considered only after the deterministic handoffs are stable.
